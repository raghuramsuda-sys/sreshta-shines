The Lion Who Found His Roar 🦁

Read this aloud — and do the voices! Be the tiny mouse, be the big lion!

Once, in a sunny green jungle, there lived a little lion cub named Leo. Leo had a very, very small roar. "Roar?" he squeaked, like a tiny mouse. One morning, Leo stood on a big rock, took a deep breath, and tried once more. "ROAR!" he roared — and the birds clapped with their wings, and the whole jungle smiled. Leo laughed and said, "My voice was inside me all along. I only had to be brave and use it!"
